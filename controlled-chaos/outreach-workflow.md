# Outreach Workflow

## Gym outreach
Use `sales/sales-outbound-strategist.md` plus `marketing/marketing-content-creator.md`. Offer a gym-specific formula challenge, sample day, member discount code, or content collab. Keep messages short and concrete.

## Influencer DMs
Use `marketing/marketing-tiktok-strategist.md` and `sales/sales-outbound-strategist.md`. Lead with a creator-specific format: formula roast, split audit, leg day formula, blind test, or gym debate. Ask for a simple yes/no.

## Customer follow-up
Use `specialized/customer-success-manager.md`. Ask what they trained, what they noticed, flavor feedback, and whether their formula matched the session. Invite UGC with a clear prompt.

## Ambassador recruitment
Use `marketing/marketing-growth-hacker.md`. Recruit college lifters and gym creators around challenges, codes, and recurring content formats.

## College/gym community outreach
Offer team/group formula audits, campus lifting club challenges, and local gym filming days.

## Discount code messages
Frame codes around action: “Build your formula before Friday’s lift,” not “use my code.”

## Required output
For each campaign: target, message, follow-up, CTA, personalization field, risk to avoid, and success metric.

## Anti-generic outreach rules
- Every gym DM must reference the gym, its lifter community, or a specific collab idea.
- Every influencer DM must propose a format, not just “we love your content.”
- Every ambassador message must include a simple action: film a formula audit, run a split challenge, collect comments, or host a gym taste test.
- Do not lead with “AI pre-workout brand.” Lead with the creator/gym scenario, then explain the quiz-built formula.
