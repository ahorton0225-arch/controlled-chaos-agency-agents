# Controlled Chaos Content Script Templates

## 7-second video
- 0–1s: visual interruption/hook.
- 1–4s: one sharp conflict or reveal.
- 4–6s: Controlled Chaos mechanism.
- 6–7s: CTA.

## 15-second video
Hook, conflict, mechanism, proof/context, CTA. Keep one idea only.

## 30-second video
Scene setup, character conflict, quiz/formula decision, payoff, objection close, CTA.

## Founder talking-head
“I noticed [specific gym problem]. So we built [mechanism]. Example: if you train [scenario], the formula should bias toward [support area]. Build yours.”

## Gym skit
Characters: generic scoop defender vs lifter with a specific training day. Use a visible prop, argument, and punchline.

## UGC testimonial
Before context, quiz moment, first-use training scenario, what felt different, restrained claim, CTA.

## Paid ad
Pattern interrupt, customer problem, product mechanism, trust builder, clear offer, CTA.

## Influencer brief
Creator context, required product beats, do-not-say claims, suggested hooks, required CTA, optional personal twist.

## Educational video
Myth, explanation, gym example, Controlled Chaos application, CTA.

## Comment-reply video
Show comment on screen, answer with a format not a lecture, invite next comment.

## Product demo
Show quiz answer, formula output logic, tub/product moment, workout scenario, CTA.
