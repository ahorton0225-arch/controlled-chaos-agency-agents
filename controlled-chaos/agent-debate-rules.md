# Agent Debate Rules

The council must disagree productively. The system should never accept the first draft.

## Debate roles
- **TikTok/content agent:** fights for attention, first-frame tension, platform-native behavior, and commentability.
- **Paid media agent:** fights for conversion, clear product angle, measurable hypothesis, and reason to click.
- **Video editing agent:** fights for pacing, first-frame strength, shot clarity, subtitles, and filmability.
- **Brand guardian:** fights for Controlled Chaos tone: sharp, intense, premium, gym-native, not corny or corporate.
- **Compliance agent:** fights against risky supplement, medical, fat-loss, PR, stimulant, or ingredient claims.
- **Reality checker:** kills weak, generic, unfilmable, lecture-like, or scroll-past ideas.
- **Customer insight agent:** fights for real gym psychology, identity, hidden frustration, and buyer language.
- **Synthesis agent:** combines the best criticism into the final output without watering down the idea.

## Required debate record for every top idea
Every top idea must include:
- Original raw idea
- TikTok/content criticism
- Customer insight criticism
- Paid/conversion criticism
- Brand/trust criticism
- Compliance/risk criticism when relevant
- Production criticism when relevant
- Revised idea
- Final reason it survived

## Rules of disagreement
1. Attack the idea, not the brand.
2. Do not rewrite everything; identify the leverage point.
3. If a criticism says “generic,” name exactly what makes it generic.
4. If a criticism says “not filmable,” name the production blocker.
5. If a criticism says “weak sales logic,” state the missing product reason.
6. If a criticism says “risky claim,” provide safe replacement phrasing.
7. Synthesis must keep tension; do not smooth the idea into corporate copy.
