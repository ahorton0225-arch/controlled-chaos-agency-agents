# Video Idea Scoring Rubric

Score every surviving idea from 1–10. Do not average weak ideas into acceptability; hard gates override the total score.

## Criteria
1. **Scroll-stop power:** Would the first frame/hook interrupt a gym-goer scrolling?
2. **Originality:** Does it avoid obvious supplement-ad angles?
3. **Gym culture relevance:** Does it use real lifter language, tension, rituals, or status?
4. **Entertainment value:** Is there a skit, game, debate, conflict, challenge, test, or visual device?
5. **Comment potential:** Does it invite an opinion, self-identification, tag, or submission?
6. **Share potential:** Would someone send this to a gym friend because it is funny, true, or useful?
7. **Sales potential:** Does it make Controlled Chaos feel worth clicking?
8. **Product clarity:** Does the viewer understand why the quiz/formula matters?
9. **Brand fit:** Does it sound sharp, premium, gym-native, and non-corporate?
10. **Filming ease:** Can one person film it this week with phone/gym/tub/cards/quiz screen?
11. **Claim safety:** Does it avoid unsupported supplement, medical, fat-loss, PR, and stimulant claims?
12. **Repeatable series potential:** Can it become a recurring format?

## Overall score
Add all 12 scores for a maximum of 120. Then classify:
- **100–120:** Film first.
- **90–99:** Strong; improve if one criterion is weak.
- **80–89:** Maybe; keep only if it fills a specific strategic gap.
- **Under 80:** Reject.

## Hard pass/fail gates
An idea cannot pass if:
- Scroll-stop power is under 7.
- Entertainment value is under 7.
- Product clarity is under 7.
- Brand fit is under 7.
- Claim safety is under 9.
- It can be summarized as “your pre should match your workout.”
- It has no visible mechanism.
- It creates polished scripts before surviving tournament, debate, scoring, and Reality Checker.
