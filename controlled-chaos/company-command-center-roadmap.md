# Company Command Center Roadmap

## Phase 1: Manual Codex workflow
Use this folder as the operating layer. Paste context, choose agents from `agent-map.md`, generate outputs, then reality-check them.

## Phase 2: Saved prompt/workflow library
Convert repeatable prompts into reusable templates for ads, website audits, outreach, support, SOPs, and product research.

## Phase 3: Replit dashboard
Create a lightweight dashboard with tabs for brand context, workflows, ad generation, website audit, outreach, support, and task planning.

## Phase 4: Ad idea library
Store concepts, hooks, scripts, scores, filming status, published links, metrics, learnings, and next iterations.

## Phase 5: Full company command center
Unify marketing, sales, website, support, operations, product research, coding, QA, and reporting. Connect real data only after manual workflows prove useful.

## What should wait
Do not build formula automation, integrations, analytics pipelines, or dashboards until the creative and operating workflows are validated manually.
