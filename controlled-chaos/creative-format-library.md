# Creative Format Library

Reusable formats for Controlled Chaos. A format is only valid when it creates a visible scene, character, conflict, test, or decision. Do not use these as titles only; each concept must show how the quiz/formula logic changes for a real lifter.

## 1. Ingredient Fantasy Draft
- **What it is:** A founder drafts formula priorities like a sports draft board.
- **Why it works:** Drafts create rankings, arguments, and easy comment bait.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** Draft pump support first for leg day, focus for finals-week lift, or lower-stim for late-night sessions.
- **Difficulty:** easy

## 2. Pre-Workout Loadout Screen
- **What it is:** A video-game stat screen for energy, pump, focus, endurance, taste, and stim.
- **Why it works:** Gamifies the quiz and makes customization instantly visible.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** Turn each quiz answer into a stat bar before a gym clip.
- **Difficulty:** medium

## 3. Gym Courtroom Trial
- **What it is:** A tub or habit is prosecuted for crimes against a specific training day.
- **Why it works:** Courtroom conflict is simple, funny, and shareable.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** Put one-size-fits-all scoops on trial and show evidence from a real split.
- **Difficulty:** medium

## 4. NPC Pre Skit
- **What it is:** A robotic supplement-ad character repeats empty claims until interrupted.
- **Why it works:** Mocks fake ad language the target customer already distrusts.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** Contrast NPC hype with direct questions about split, caffeine, and goals.
- **Difficulty:** easy

## 5. Fake Podcast Debate
- **What it is:** Two exaggerated gym opinions debate stim, pump, taste, or AI.
- **Why it works:** Podcast framing lets one person play multiple characters.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** Stage “high stim is a personality” vs “build around the session.”
- **Difficulty:** easy

## 6. Street Interview Outside Gym
- **What it is:** Ask lifters one sharp question before/after training.
- **Why it works:** Feels native, social, and proof-oriented.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** Ask “what are you training and what should your pre prioritize?”
- **Difficulty:** medium

## 7. Build My Leg Day Formula
- **What it is:** Recurring series building formulas for specific leg-day profiles.
- **Why it works:** Series structure trains the audience to comment their own split.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** Build for quad day, glute day, heavy squat day, or high-volume accessories.
- **Difficulty:** easy

## 8. Coach Whiteboard Breakdown
- **What it is:** A coach explains formula priorities on a whiteboard.
- **Why it works:** Turns product logic into a teachable visual.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** Draw split, goal, tolerance, and formula emphasis boxes.
- **Difficulty:** easy

## 9. Gym Bro Argument
- **What it is:** Two lifters argue over a specific pre-workout belief.
- **Why it works:** Conflict beats explanation and invites comments.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** Argue whether a 9 p.m. lift needs the same scoop as Saturday legs.
- **Difficulty:** easy

## 10. Blind Test
- **What it is:** A blindfolded or hidden-label comparison with clear caveats.
- **Why it works:** Testing language feels less like an ad.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** Compare taste expectations or training-day fit, not medical outcomes.
- **Difficulty:** medium

## 11. Myth-Busting Challenge
- **What it is:** One myth gets tested or explained in under 30 seconds.
- **Why it works:** Challenges create stakes and structure.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** Bust “more caffeine always means better pre” using tolerance scenarios.
- **Difficulty:** easy

## 12. Founder Experiment
- **What it is:** Founder runs a small, honest experiment.
- **Why it works:** Founder energy builds trust and makes AI feel human.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** Try two formula priorities on two different training days and explain limits.
- **Difficulty:** medium

## 13. Ingredient War Room
- **What it is:** A mock command center assigns priorities to a lifter profile.
- **Why it works:** Makes formulation feel engineered and premium.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** Use sticky notes for pump, energy, focus, endurance, taste.
- **Difficulty:** medium

## 14. Locker Room Debate
- **What it is:** Fast debate in a gym corner or locker-room-style setup.
- **Why it works:** Casual setting feels gym-native.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** Debate whether caffeine, pump, or focus wins for a named workout.
- **Difficulty:** easy

## 15. Formula Roast
- **What it is:** Respectfully roast a submitted routine or stack.
- **Why it works:** Roasts drive comments without needing ad spend.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** Invite lifters to submit split + stim tolerance for a formula audit.
- **Difficulty:** easy

## 16. Tier List
- **What it is:** Rank formula priorities, flavors, or training-day needs.
- **Why it works:** Tier lists are familiar and argument-friendly.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** Rank what matters most for legs, pull, push, conditioning, late-night lifts.
- **Difficulty:** easy

## 17. Pre-Workout Audit
- **What it is:** Audit a current routine against a lifter’s workout.
- **Why it works:** Turns objections into consultative sales.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** Ask training day, time, tolerance, goal, and flavor; output gaps.
- **Difficulty:** easy

## 18. POV Skit
- **What it is:** Viewer is placed inside a specific gym scenario.
- **Why it works:** POV formats are fast and relatable.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** POV: you took a high-stim scoop for a 10 p.m. arm day.
- **Difficulty:** easy

## 19. Workout Split Comparison
- **What it is:** Compare how different splits should change formula emphasis.
- **Why it works:** Shows personalization without saying “personalized” lazily.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** PPL vs bro split vs athlete conditioning in three columns.
- **Difficulty:** medium

## 20. Gym Game Show
- **What it is:** Audience guesses formula priorities from lifter profiles.
- **Why it works:** Interactive guessing drives comments and retention.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** Reveal answers after viewers pick A/B/C.
- **Difficulty:** medium

## 21. Comment-Bait Challenge
- **What it is:** Ask viewers for splits, objections, or routines to transform.
- **Why it works:** Makes the comment section the content engine.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** “Drop your split + caffeine tolerance; I’ll build the formula priorities.”
- **Difficulty:** easy

## 22. Creator Testimonial Brief
- **What it is:** A creator documents one training scenario and formula fit.
- **Why it works:** Keeps UGC specific instead of scripted.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** Ask creator to show quiz inputs, workout, and honest first-session notes.
- **Difficulty:** easy

## 23. Gas Station Pre Intervention
- **What it is:** A phone-filmable scene where gas station pre intervention becomes the device for explaining a lifter-specific formula decision.
- **Why it works:** It gives the viewer a named situation, a visible prop, and a reason to comment instead of hearing a generic supplement promise.
- **Controlled Chaos use:** Tie the scene to one concrete profile: workout split, training time, goal, stim tolerance, flavor preference, and CTA to build the formula.
- **Difficulty:** hard
