# Reality Checker Workflow

Act like a harsh creative director who protects Controlled Chaos from boring supplement content.

## Review gates
Score each idea 1–5 on:
1. Hook interruption
2. Specific training context
3. Visual mechanism
4. Comment/share potential
5. Sales clarity
6. Ease of filming
7. Brand fit
8. Claim safety

## Automatic rejection flags
- Generic claims: “best pre,” “next level,” “game changer,” “personalized for you” with no proof mechanism.
- Weak hook: explains the product before creating curiosity.
- Unclear product angle: viewer cannot say why Controlled Chaos exists.
- Too hard to film: requires actors, permits, heavy VFX, pro studio, or expensive props.
- Category sameness: sounds like every supplement brand.
- AI-sounding script: polished corporate language, no human rhythm, no gym-native specificity.
- Unsupported claims: guarantees PRs, fat loss, medical outcomes, or extreme stimulant effects.

## Regeneration rule
If any idea scores below 4 on visual mechanism or hook interruption, rewrite it using one of these mechanisms: courtroom, fantasy draft, audit, roast, street interview, founder experiment, whiteboard, blind test, tier list, game loadout, NPC skit, locker-room debate.

## Final approval standard
Approved ideas must be specific, visual, executable with a phone, and tied to a real customer scenario.

## Sales-logic rejection
Reject any idea if it only earns attention but does not make the viewer understand why the quiz/formula system matters. The approved rewrite must include: customer profile, missing question, formula priority, and CTA.

## Corny/corporate language ban
Flag and rewrite phrases like “unlock your potential,” “take performance to the next level,” “game changer,” “revolutionary,” “premium solution,” “optimize your lifestyle,” and “fuel your journey.” Replace them with concrete gym language: late lift, leg day, top set, pump, crash, flavor, caffeine tolerance, split, and build your formula.

## Filmability test
If the concept cannot be filmed with a phone, gym, product tub, laptop/phone quiz screen, whiteboard/cards, and one or two people, mark it **too heavy** and simplify the format.
