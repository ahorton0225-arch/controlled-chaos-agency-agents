# Website Audit Workflow

## Team
- `design/design-ux-architect.md` for structure and flow.
- `marketing/marketing-content-creator.md` for conversion copy.
- `product/product-manager.md` for product explanation and quiz logic.
- `design/design-brand-guardian.md` for brand consistency.
- `testing/testing-reality-checker.md` for objections and weak claims.

## Audit sequence
1. **Hero clarity:** Can a cold visitor understand custom AI-generated pre-workout in five seconds?
2. **Quiz CTA:** Is the primary action obvious above the fold and repeated naturally?
3. **Mechanism:** Does the page explain how training style, split, goals, caffeine tolerance, and preferences influence the formula?
4. **Objection handling:** Address AI skepticism, ingredient confidence, price, taste, shipping, and stimulant tolerance.
5. **Conversion copy:** Replace vague hype with training-specific examples.
6. **Checkout confidence:** Add transparent expectations, support, refund/replacement language, and trust builders.
7. **Reality check:** Remove unsupported claims and generic supplement language.

## Output
Return prioritized fixes, exact copy blocks, quiz-flow improvements, FAQ additions, CTA variants, and a before/after conversion rationale.
