# Test Run: 10 Controlled Chaos Ad Ideas

## 1. Ingredient Fantasy Draft
- **Hook:** “First pick in the leg day pre-workout draft is not caffeine.”
- **Mechanism:** Fantasy draft board for pump, focus, endurance, taste.
- **Script:** Founder drafts ingredients/categories for a brutal leg day while rejecting “random scoop energy.”
- **Shot list:** Whiteboard draft grid, product tub, squat rack, final formula card.
- **CTA:** Build your formula.
- **Scores:** View 9, Comments 8, Sales 8, Ease 8.

## 2. Gym Courtroom: The Gas Station Scoop Trial
- **Hook:** “The defendant is charged with ruining push day.”
- **Mechanism:** Courtroom skit with judge, accused tub, evidence labels.
- **Script:** Prosecutor shows crash, bad taste, no training context; Controlled Chaos presents quiz-based formula logic.
- **Shot list:** Bench as judge stand, tub on trial, evidence cards, verdict overlay.
- **CTA:** Put your pre on trial. Build yours.
- **Scores:** View 9, Comments 9, Sales 7, Ease 7.

## 3. Pre-Workout Loadout Screen
- **Hook:** “If pre-workout had a loadout screen, this is what leg day gets.”
- **Mechanism:** Video game loadout edit.
- **Script:** Select workout split, stim tolerance, pump/focus priority, flavor; reveal loadout.
- **Shot list:** Phone quiz taps, gym clips, overlay stat bars, tub hero shot.
- **CTA:** Build your loadout.
- **Scores:** View 10, Comments 8, Sales 9, Ease 6.

## 4. Founder Whiteboard Bet
- **Hook:** “Give me your split and caffeine tolerance. I’ll tell you what your pre should prioritize.”
- **Mechanism:** Founder challenge/comment reply.
- **Script:** Founder breaks down a commenter’s PPL split and late-night training constraints.
- **Shot list:** Comment screenshot, whiteboard, formula priorities, CTA.
- **CTA:** Comment your split or take the quiz.
- **Scores:** View 8, Comments 10, Sales 8, Ease 9.

## 5. NPC Pre-Workout Skit
- **Hook:** “NPC supplement ad voice just spawned in the gym.”
- **Mechanism:** Character parody vs direct founder correction.
- **Script:** NPC says empty claims; founder interrupts with actual training-specific formula questions.
- **Shot list:** Robotic ad voice, jump cut, quiz screen, gym set.
- **CTA:** Escape NPC pre. Build yours.
- **Scores:** View 8, Comments 8, Sales 7, Ease 9.

## 6. Formula Roast
- **Hook:** “Drop your current pre-workout routine. I’ll roast it respectfully.”
- **Mechanism:** Comment bait audit.
- **Script:** Roast a fake routine: high-stim scoop before 9 p.m. hypertrophy day; suggest better priorities.
- **Shot list:** Comment overlay, founder reaction, whiteboard fix, tub.
- **CTA:** Take the quiz before your next lift.
- **Scores:** View 8, Comments 10, Sales 7, Ease 9.

## 7. Two Lifters, Same Scoop Problem
- **Hook:** “One guy is deadlifting. One guy is doing arms. Why are they taking the same pre?”
- **Mechanism:** Split-screen comparison.
- **Script:** Show two training days needing different emphasis; Controlled Chaos asks the missing questions.
- **Shot list:** Deadlift clip, curl clip, same tub gag, quiz answers, formula priorities.
- **CTA:** Build around how you train.
- **Scores:** View 7, Comments 7, Sales 9, Ease 8.

## 8. Locker Room Debate
- **Hook:** “Locker room debate: is caffeine a personality?”
- **Mechanism:** Fast debate between high-stim bro and pump-focused lifter.
- **Script:** Characters argue; founder reframes around tolerance and session type.
- **Shot list:** Locker/gym corner, two angles, debate captions, quiz reveal.
- **CTA:** Pick your tolerance in the quiz.
- **Scores:** View 8, Comments 9, Sales 8, Ease 8.

## 9. Supplement Label Autopsy
- **Hook:** “We are not reading this label. We are performing an autopsy.”
- **Mechanism:** Detective/medical examiner parody.
- **Script:** Identify vague claims and missing training context, then show Controlled Chaos formula logic.
- **Shot list:** Gloves optional, label close-ups, red string notes, formula card.
- **CTA:** Build a formula with context.
- **Scores:** View 8, Comments 8, Sales 8, Ease 7.

## 10. Gym Game Show: Guess the Formula
- **Hook:** “Guess which formula belongs to leg day, finals week, and a 9 p.m. lift.”
- **Mechanism:** Game show quiz.
- **Script:** Host gives three lifter profiles; audience guesses formula priority before reveal.
- **Shot list:** Index cards, buzzer sound, gym background, reveal overlays.
- **CTA:** Take the quiz and get your profile.
- **Scores:** View 9, Comments 9, Sales 8, Ease 8.

## Top 5 to film first
1. **Founder Whiteboard Bet** — strongest comment engine and easiest to film.
2. **Pre-Workout Loadout Screen** — strongest sales explanation and visual identity.
3. **Ingredient Fantasy Draft** — highly repeatable series format.
4. **Gym Courtroom Trial** — best shareable skit format.
5. **Gym Game Show** — strong comments, easy props, recurring format.

## Summary
These ideas avoid naked generic claims by using concrete mechanisms: draft, courtroom, loadout, whiteboard challenge, NPC parody, roast, split-screen contrast, debate, autopsy, and game show. Each can be filmed with a phone, gym access, product tub, whiteboard/cards, and basic editing.
